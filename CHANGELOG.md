# Merchant Map

## 1.2.3

- While a patrolling vendor is your marked vendor, their route now stays on the world map and also shows on the minimap.
- A search that finds no items or service NPCs now lists vendors whose name or title matches. Click one to mark and target them.
- Profession trainers whose title doesn't name their profession are now recorded from what they teach when you open their training window.
- Updated vendor data:
  - 19 more vendors and 16 more trainers and other service NPCs checked in game, among them the Everlook vendors, engineering trainer Kaylaena Springwhisper and ravasaur trainer Mor'vek.
  - Also new: Chagrak Hammerstrike (repairs) and trainer Hnaz Blunderflame in Mount Hyjal.
  - Arille Azuregaze, Aemara and Paige are shown for the Alliance only.
  - Vendors that only stand in one place, like those in Redridge and the city recipe vendors, no longer say they have more than one spawn.

## 1.2.2

- Vendors that walk around or spawn in more than one place, like Zixil and Pexmit, now say so in item and map pin tooltips. Hovering their map pin shows their patrol route as a line, or their spawn spots as dots. Items with a single vendor show "Only sold by".
- Updated vendor data:
  - 65 more vendors and 71 more trainers, flight masters and other service NPCs checked in game, among them the vendors and flight master Fayran Elthas in Mount Hyjal.
  - Also new: Pexmit and innkeeper Ortak Lomgok in Mount Hyjal, and journeyman blacksmith Gur'dok.
  - Gilliwigs is shown for the Alliance only again.

## 1.2.1

- Updated vendor data:
  - 13 more vendors, among them Jenna the Mustard Queen in Stranglethorn Vale, a bartender and a chef in the Wetlands, Zippie Fizzbolt the Everlook Quartermaster, Dala Fairmaw the Quartermaster in Mount Hyjal, and Dorb's rare goods.
  - 3 more bankers, 4 more innkeepers, Auctioneer Quickcoin in the Barrens and Master Mycologist Shurome in Felwood.
  - Now shown for both factions: Mika Darby, Gilliwigs, Xaphod Bizznox, several vendors in Mount Hyjal, and the mining and fishing trainers Grungle and Krix.

## 1.2.0

- The window's title now shows the version number.
- Fixed a Lua error when resizing the window with the mouse over an item.

## 1.1.12
